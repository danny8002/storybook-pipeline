# The Legend of the White Snake

A white snake named Bai Suzhen lived on Mount Emei.

For a thousand years, she learned to use magic.

One day, her magic made her into a woman.

But Bai Suzhen felt lonely. She wanted someone to love.

She went to Hangzhou with Xiaoqing, a green snake friend.

They walked by West Lake. Rain began to fall.

A kind man named Xu Xian gave them his umbrella.

"Come to our house tomorrow," said Bai Suzhen. "Get it then."

He came for it. They drank tea and talked.

They fell in love. Soon, Bai Suzhen and Xu Xian married.

They moved to Zhenjiang and opened a small medicine shop.

They helped sick people. Poor people got medicine for free.

A monk named Fahai watched them. He knew her secret.

"Snake spirits must not live with people," he said.

At his temple, Fahai told Xu Xian, "Your wife is a snake!"

"She is kind!" said Xu Xian. "She helps sick people!"

"Give her realgar wine at the festival," said Fahai. "You'll see."

At the Dragon Boat Festival, Xu Xian gave her the wine.

She drank it and felt sick. She went to bed.

Xu Xian brought soup. A big white snake lay there!

He dropped the bowl. He was so scared that he died.

Bai Suzhen became a woman again. She held him and cried.

Only a magic plant on Mount Kunlun could save him.

She was going to have a baby. Still, she must go.

Xiaoqing stayed with Xu Xian. Bai Suzhen flew through a storm.

High on the snowy mountain, she picked the magic plant.

Two guards fought her. She fell but held it tight.

A wise old man stopped them. "Let her take it."

She flew home and made medicine from the magic plant.

Xu Xian drank it. His eyes opened. He was alive!

Xiaoqing showed him white cloth. Had that been the snake?

But Fahai took Xu Xian away and kept him inside the temple.

Bai Suzhen and Xiaoqing came by boat. "Let him go!"

Fahai said no. Bai Suzhen sent river water at the gates.

Her baby would come soon. In pain, she turned back.

A young monk helped Xu Xian get out of the temple.

At the bridge, he found his wife. "Snake or not, I love you."

Their son, Xu Mengjiao, was born. Bai Suzhen held him close.

Then Fahai came. His magic bowl shone with bright light.

The light trapped her under Leifeng Pagoda, a tall stone tower.

"Stay until the lake dries and this tower falls!" said Fahai.

At home, Xu Xian told Mengjiao about his kind mother.

Mengjiao wanted her back. He studied hard as he grew.

Twenty years passed. Mengjiao won the top prize for his studies.

He and his father went to the tower by West Lake.

Mengjiao knelt and prayed. Heaven heard him. The stone door opened.

Bai Suzhen stepped out. Her husband and son ran to her.

They held each other close. At last, they were together.
